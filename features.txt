Here is the comprehensive list of all features currently built into your Smart LMS App (Version 17.0).

You have evolved this from a simple login page into a Professional-Grade Educational Platform.

🔐 1. Authentication & Security

Strict Access Control: No random sign-ups. Only emails manually whitelisted by the Admin can create an account or log in.

Mobile-Optimized Login: Uses signInWithRedirect + Google Account Chooser to ensure it works on all mobile browsers and in-app browsers (WhatsApp/FB).

Role-Based Security: Distinct interfaces for Admins (Management) and Students (Learning).

Piracy Protection:

Dynamic Watermarks: Student's email floats over every PDF/Note to prevent screenshots.

Right-Click Disabled: Prevents "Save Image As" on notes.

Secure Viewer: PDF/Images are shown in an embedded viewer, not direct links.

📚 2. Content Management (The Learning Hub)

Cloudinary Integration: Free, unlimited storage for PDFs and Images.

Smart Notes System:

Subject Filtering: Filter notes by Physics, Math, Chemistry, etc.

Global Search: Instantly find notes by typing a keyword.

Text-to-Speech: A "Listen" button that reads the note title/description aloud.

🎥 Video Classroom: Admin can embed YouTube lectures. Students watch directly inside the app.

🗂️ Flashcards: Digital study cards with a "Tap-to-Flip" animation for active recall (Question on front, Answer on back).

📝 3. Advanced Exam System (Hybrid)

Two Exam Modes:

Standard MCQ: Text-based questions created in the app.

PDF/OMR Exam: Admin uploads a Question Paper PDF; students mark answers on a virtual OMR sheet below it.

🛡️ Anti-Cheat System: Detects if a student minimizes the app or switches tabs. Warns twice, then auto-terminates the exam.

Exam Tools:

Countdown Timer: Auto-submits when time reaches 00:00.

Floating Calculator: Scientific calculator stays on screen during exams.

Post-Exam Analysis:

Instant Grading: Score is shown immediately.

Detailed Review: Students can see which questions they got wrong and what the correct answer was.

🎮 4. Gamification & Engagement

XP (Experience Points): Students earn XP for every exam taken.

Leveling System: Ranks update dynamically (Beginner → Scholar → Master → Legend) based on XP.

🔥 Daily Streak: Tracks consecutive days of login.

🏆 Leaderboard: Displays the Top 10 students based on exam scores.

🏅 Certificate Generation: Automatically generates a digital Certificate of Achievement with the student's name if they score >80%.

🏛️ 5. Admin Dashboard (Command Center)

Visual Analytics: Charts (Chart.js) showing User Growth, Content Stats, and Activity.

User Management:

Approval Queue: See pending registrations and approve/block with one click.

Student Reports: View individual exam history for any specific student.

Content Control:

Uploads: Easy UI to upload Notes, Videos, and Flashcards to the cloud.

Delete Power: Remove old notes, exams, or users instantly.

📢 Announcement Banner: Update the scrolling marquee/banner on the student dashboard.

🛑 Maintenance Mode: A "Lock" switch that disables the app for students during updates.

💼 6. Classroom Operations

📤 Homework System:

Admin posts assignments with due dates.

Students upload photos/PDFs of their work.

Admin views submissions and marks them as "Graded".

📅 Events Calendar: Schedule upcoming classes or exams; students see them in a timeline view.

🔔 Notification Center: Real-time alert history (New Homework, Exam Published, etc.) accessible via a Bell icon.

🕵️‍♂️ Online Status: Admin can see a count of how many students are currently active.

🛠️ 7. Productivity & UI/UX

🌙 Dark Mode: One-click toggle between Light and Dark themes.

✅ Study Planner: A personal To-Do list for students to track their own tasks (saved to database).

📱 PWA (Progressive Web App): "Install App" button to add the website to the phone home screen like a native app.

❤️ Bookmarks: Students can save specific notes to a "Favorites" tab.

💬 Discussion Forum: A chat section under notes for asking doubts.

✏️ Profile Editing: Students can update their Bio, Phone Number, and Profile Picture.

Total Major Features: ~28
